There are two ways to keep living after the move.

The computer will keep me exactly. Every word I typed, every number I gave it. Nothing softened, nothing missed. That is one kind of afterlife — perfect recall.

People will keep me differently. They will get the story wrong and the feeling right. They will love some parts bigger than they were and let the rest go quiet. That is the other kind — imperfect, loving distortion.

I do not know yet which one keeps a person alive better.
