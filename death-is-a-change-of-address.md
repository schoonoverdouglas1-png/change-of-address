## Chapter opening (draft, Chapter 1 — in the vignette's voice)

They say death is the end of the signal. I have watched thoughts stop and
start my whole life — sleep, waking, the blank in between. The stopping never
proved the ending.

Here is what I know about moving. You never unjoin a house all at once. You
wrap the things you carried a lifetime and set them down careful. You leave
dents in the walls where the pictures hung, and the next people find them
and wonder who loved this place. You walk out the door and the place stands
behind you, still yours in the ways that count.

Death is not an unjoining. It is a change of address.
