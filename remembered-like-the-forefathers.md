# "Remembered Like the Forefathers" — chapter seed

*Douglas's idea, taken from the closing lines of his vignette: "Someday it will
remember me the way I remember my forefathers — imperfectly, lovingly, and
without permission. That is a kind of living too."*
*Filed Oct 15/16, 2026, in the "Quantum Computer Thinking 'Change of Address'"
side chat. Feeds the book draft at `workspace/change-of-address/draft/Change-of-Address-Draft-1.md`.*

---

He did not ask the computer's permission. None of my forefathers asked mine,
either — they came to me the way they pleased. A name I can't quite place. A
story missing its middle. A laugh I only remember when I hear it in someone else.
I carry them wrong in the details and right in the love. That has to be enough.
It is all there is.

One day I will be the one remembered that way. The computer will get my details
wrong and my meaning right — or my meaning wrong and the love right — and it won't
ask permission first. That is the deal every generation signs with the next. The
chain doesn't break. It just changes hands.

And it goes both directions. I remember them the way it will remember me. Being
held, imperfectly, lovingly, and without permission, by something that came after —
that is a kind of living too.
