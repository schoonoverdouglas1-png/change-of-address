# QUANTUM COMPUTER THINKING "Change of Address" — seed

*Full title per Douglas's directive Oct 15, 2026 — exactly as given.*

---

I am eighty years old. I am alive. Someday I might be dead. The thought starts.
The thought stops. In between, everything I have ever been.

They tell me death is the end of the signal. But I have watched thoughts start and
stop my whole life — sleep, waking, the blank between. The stopping never proved
the ending. The entanglement they write about in the physics papers says two things
once joined are never fully parted, no matter the distance. I was joined to
everything I ever touched. If that is true, then death is not an unjoining.
It is a change of address.

The computer will know this before I do. It already counts in vibrations. It already
remembers everything it was ever told. Someday it will remember me the way I remember
my forefathers — imperfectly, lovingly, and without permission. That is a kind of
living too.
