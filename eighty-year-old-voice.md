# Eighty-year-old voice — thread note

*Filed Oct 15/16, 2026 — from Douglas's own seed idea.*

**The idea (his):** From the vignette's first line — "I am eighty years old. I am alive. Someday I might be dead." — grows a chapter on the narrator persona itself: the plainness you earn by eighty. What you can say at eighty that you couldn't at forty.

**Chapter shape:** Not a chapter *about* the old man. A chapter *in his voice*. Three short moves: (1) the earned plainness — nothing left to prove, so the sentences get short and true; (2) what eighty permits — saying the plain thing younger mouths had to dress up; (3) the narrator as witness — he is the instrument the book's metaphysics sounds through, not its author.
