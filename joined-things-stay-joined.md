*Filed Oct 15/16, 2026. This chapter is Douglas's idea, seeded from the entanglement
passage: "two things once joined are never fully parted... I was joined to
everything I ever touched."*

**The chapter:** the metaphor with teeth — joined things stay joined, no matter the
distance.

**Honest flag (must be carried):** quantum entanglement does not establish any of
this in physics. The book's claim is metaphor, not physics — Douglas's metaphysics,
labeled as such. This flag rides with the chapter everywhere.

**Voice:** plainspoken, like the eighty-year-old vignette. No physics lectures, no
grand machinery — just the plain truth of the joining.
