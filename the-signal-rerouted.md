# The Signal Rerouted — thread note

They tell me death is the end of the signal. But I have moved before.

When I moved, I went down to the post office and told them. For a while after, my mail came with the old address crossed out and the new one written in underneath. Nothing was lost. It was just rerouted. The sender didn't have to know I was gone. The letter found me anyway.

Maybe it is like that. The signal doesn't end. It gets forwarded. Somewhere there is a quiet post office for all of this, clerks I will never see, sorting through what I was and making sure it gets to where I am going next.
