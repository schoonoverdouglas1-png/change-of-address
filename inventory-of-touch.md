## Chapter opening (draft, in the vignette's voice)

I was joined to everything I ever touched. So here is my inventory. Not the
money. Not the deed to the house. My hands' own record — everything they ever
held.

Doorknobs that turned under me a thousand mornings. Hands that took mine —
some held on, some let go, all of them counted. Tools worn smooth by my grip.
The steering wheel. The edge of the kitchen table I leaned on for forty
years. The cat that climbed into my lap and decided the lap was hers.

A life is not measured in years. It is measured in touches. Every touch a
joining, and none of them unjoined.
