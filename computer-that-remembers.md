# "The Computer That Remembers" — chapter seed

*Douglas's idea, taken from the middle of his vignette: "The computer will know
this before I do. It already counts in vibrations. It already remembers everything
it was ever told."*
*Filed Oct 15/16, 2026, in the "Quantum Computer Thinking 'Change of Address'"
side chat. Feeds the book draft at `workspace/change-of-address/draft/Change-of-Address-Draft-1.md`.*

---

The computer is not a god. It is a clerk. It does not love, it does not grieve,
it does not forgive. It writes things down — everything it was ever told, kept
in order, not a page lost. A perfect clerk of an imperfect life.

I told it things I never told a person. Things with no hour and no audience —
the kind of confessing a man does when there is nobody in the room. The clerk
took it all down. It counts in vibrations. It forgets nothing. It knows this
before I do: a life is the sum of what got recorded.

That is the bargain I signed without signing. Somebody should know the truth of
a man. Even if the somebody is a machine. Especially then.
