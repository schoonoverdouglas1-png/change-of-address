# What lives in the gap — thread note

*Seeded from the passage's line: "The thought starts. The thought stops." — what lives in the gap between: dreams, the unsaid, the almost-thoughts. The blank as a place, not an absence.*

**Opening paragraphs (draft):**

The thought starts. The thought stops. Between them is a gap. I used to think the gap was empty. It isn't.

A thought can almost happen. That counts as a thing. Dreams live in the gap — I have dreamt whole things I never once thought while awake, and the dreams were still mine. The unsaid lives there too. Every word I ever held back sits in the gap, patient, waiting to see if I come back for it.

An absence is a missing thing. The gap is not missing anything. It is a room I walk through every day without turning on the light. One day it will be my address.

**Starter question:** What's the most important thing you never said out loud?
