# Reader as Next Address — thread note

---

A book is a change of address. Not a record of the move. The move itself.

You write it down — everything you carried, packed careful between covers. Then a stranger opens it and you walk in. You take up residence in their head. You live there for the length of the reading, thinking your thoughts in their voice, or theirs in yours. For a few hours, two people share one address.

The body only ever had one address. The book can have a thousand. Ten thousand. Every reader is a new place where you still live — still thinking, still remembering, still turning pages. Long after the first address stands empty, there are lights on at all the others.
